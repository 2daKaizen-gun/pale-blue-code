## The predictor has nothing to point at

Re-run today over every searched star (now 188, three pools):

```
fit: log(floor) = -9.5613 + 0.3634·Tmag   (n=188, in-sample R^2 = 0.170)
held-out skill (train 94 / test 94): Earth-size precision 1.00 · recall 0.20 · median |err| 2.02 R⊕
candidate pool: pool_s0102.json (90 unsearched) — predicted Earth-size: 0
```

The predictor is still right when it says Earth-size (precision 1.00), but it now says it about **no**
remaining sector-102 star, and its recall has slipped from 0.25 to **0.20** as the searched set grew.
Its ranking among the non-positives is what's left, and at R² 0.17 that is not guidance: the top of it
is bright Tmag ~6.4 stars at R★ ~1.1, predicted ~3.3 R⊕. Following it would have spent the fetch budget
on stars no one expects to reach Earth size.

So this batch follows the **fetch plan's own order** — smallest achievable planet first. That is the
order that produced most of the 12 Earth-size-sensitive targets, including TIC 107548305, the star the
predictor had called non-Earth. The predictor keeps its one job, flagging positives; with none to flag,
it steps aside rather than being dressed up as a ranking it cannot support.

## What "quiet" is worth here — the honest reading

The plan's smallest-first order has now walked past the pool's smallest dwarfs: this batch is all
R★ 0.59–0.65 R☉. At that size, and on these curves, **none reached Earth size**; the sharpest,
TIC 124768372, bottoms out at 2.35 R⊕. And four of the six "quiet" stars had measured floors of
1.4–5.4% — curves so noisy that the smallest planet they could have revealed is **8–16 R⊕**. For those
four, "quiet" rules out Neptunes and Saturns, and says nothing at all about anything smaller.

Achieved completeness over the 18 searched sector-102 stars, from each curve's own measured floor:

added none. That is the expected cost of widening into larger dwarfs, and it sharpens what sub-11-27
already hinted: the widening lever pays off on the *small* end of a pool, which sector 102 has now
mostly spent.
